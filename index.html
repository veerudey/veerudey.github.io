🧠 What is AI?

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence — it means giving machines or computers the ability to think and learn like humans. AI allows systems to understand, reason, and make decisions automatically.

🤖 Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) a technology that creates a fully immersive, computer-generated environment that users can interact with.

Users wear VR headset that block out the real world and replace it with a digital environment, giving the sensation of being in a different place or world. They can interact with the virtual environment using controllers or body movement, enhancing the feeling of presence within the digital world. This technology is often used in gaming, simulations, and training applications.
