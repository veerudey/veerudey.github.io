🧠 What is AI?

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence — it means giving machines or computers the ability to think and learn like humans. AI allows systems to understand, reason, and make decisions automatically.

_______________

🕶️ Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that creates a fully immersive, computer-generated environment that users can interact with.

Users wear a VR headset that blocks out the real world and replaces it with a digital one, giving the sensation of being in a different place. This technology is used in gaming, simulations, and training.

_______________

🎮 Where is Virtual Reality Being Used?

Gaming: VR is used for bewitching gaming experiences. You can put on a VR headset and feel as if you are inside the game — exploring magical worlds or battling virtual monsters.

_______________

Education: VR can make learning more engaging. Students can take virtual field trips, explore the human body in 3D, or even travel to space to learn about planets and stars.
